Signet Verify

Check a provenance seal for agent-written content.

A seal claims three things: this exact text was hashed, a wallet signed that hash, and that wallet is registered to a verified unique human. This page re-runs each check separately, in your browser, and reports each one in words. Nothing here is taken on trust from the seal itself.

A seal that has not been checked here claims only that it was
  sealed. Registration is read live from the AgentBook registry on World Chain
  at the moment of the check, and the check time is always shown. A seal with
  no anchor says so: its timing is the signer's claim, not a chain proof.
  Human approved is a separate claim from sealed, and is reported separately.
  Sample seals use a published test key and a recorded registration fixture,
  and are labelled as samples.
